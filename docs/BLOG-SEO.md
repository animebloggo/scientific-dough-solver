# Journal and SEO

Eight posts live under `/blog`, built to rank for three brand terms and five
informational terms. This is what was built, what needs your input, and how to
add the next post.

## Before this goes live

Two items need a human decision. Both are one line changes.

### 1. Confirm the canonical domain

`SITE_URL` in `src/lib/seo.ts` is set to `https://dissolvemydough.com`, taken
from the support address in the footer. It drives every canonical tag, every
Open Graph URL and the sitemap. **If the storefront lives on a different domain,
change that one constant.** A canonical pointing at the wrong host tells search
engines to index the wrong URL.

### 2. Fill in the competitor figures

`src/content/competitor.ts` holds the comparison rows for the Dough Dissolve
article. Every `theirs` field is `null`, which renders as "Not published" in the
table rather than as a guess. The article is publishable in that state and the
footnote explains the policy.

To fill one in, open their own product page or label, paste the figure exactly
as they state it, and record the URL and date in `source`. Do not fill these
from memory, a review site or a competitor deck. The comparison table in the
article is generated from that file, so editing it updates the post.

## What is wired up

- **Per page meta** built by `buildMeta()` in `src/lib/seo.ts`: title,
  description, keywords, robots with `max-image-preview:large`, full Open Graph
  and Twitter cards, and `article:*` tags on posts.
- **Canonical URLs** on every blog page.
- **Structured data** in `src/lib/structured-data.ts`, rendered server side:
  `Organization` and `WebSite` site wide, plus `BlogPosting`, `BreadcrumbList`,
  `FAQPage` and `HowTo` per post. The `Organization` entry carries
  `alternateName: "Dissolve My Dough"`, which is the signal that tells search
  engines the two names are one company.
- **FAQ blocks** use native `<details>` rather than a JavaScript accordion, so
  the answers are in the server rendered HTML and match the FAQ structured data.
- **sitemap.xml and robots.txt** generated by `scripts/generate-sitemap.mjs`
  from the real post registry. It runs automatically on `npm run dev` and
  `npm run build`, so it cannot go stale. Shopify product URLs are fetched at
  runtime and are not in the sitemap yet.
- **Internal linking**: every post links to two or three others plus the shop,
  the header carries a Journal link, and the footer links four cornerstone posts
  from every page on the site.

## Adding a post

1. Copy any file in `src/content/posts/` and edit it. The body is structured
   blocks rather than markdown, typed in `src/content/blog-types.ts`. Inline
   text supports `**bold**` and `[label](/path)` only.
2. Register it in `src/content/blog.ts`.
3. Add a hero image and its alt text in `src/content/postImages.ts`.
4. Run `npm run dev`. The sitemap regenerates on its own.

Keep `seoTitle` near 60 characters, `description` between 140 and 160, and give
every `h2` block a stable `id`, since those ids are the table of contents
anchors and the jump links search engines can surface.

## Two things worth doing next

- **Author bylines are team level** (`AUTHORS` in `src/content/blog-types.ts`).
  Real named people with real credentials are a stronger authorship signal.
  Swap them in once the chemist and head baker are happy to be named.
- **Site branding still says Dissolve My Dough** in the header, footer and the
  non blog page titles. The journal leads with After Dough and the structured
  data declares the rebrand, so the two are deliberately out of step until you
  decide to flip the rest of the site.
